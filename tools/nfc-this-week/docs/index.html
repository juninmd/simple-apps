Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Straight answers for non-technical users and everyday bill splitting.

❓ What is an NFC-e receipt?

NFC-e (Nota Fiscal de Consumidor Eletrônica) is the standard digital receipt issued by restaurants, supermarkets, and shops across Brazil. Every paper receipt has a square QR code printed at the bottom.

❓ Do I need to create an account or sign in?

No. You never need to enter an email, password, or phone number. There are no logins, subscriptions, or accounts. Just open the page, scan, and split.

❓ Where does my data go? Is it stored in the cloud?

Nowhere. The app operates 100% locally on your device. Neither your purchase history, CPF, nor bill amounts are sent to any server or cloud database.

❓ What if the receipt is wrinkled or the camera can't scan it?

You can either smooth out the paper under good lighting, hold the camera steady about 15cm away, or paste the 44-digit numeric key printed on the receipt directly into the app.

❓ How does the 10% service fee or tip get divided?

The app automatically distributes service fees and discounts proportionally based on what each person consumed, ensuring complete fairness without manual math.

❓ How do I send the result to my friends?

After reviewing the split, click "Copy WhatsApp Text". Then open your group chat in WhatsApp and paste. Everyone gets a breakdown of their items and their exact PIX amount.
